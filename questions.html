Questions to ask before you load a card

Last updated 23 November 2026. Not financial, tax, or legal advice.

Use the issuer’s official fee table, then sense-check the totals against an independent calculator.

Once you have the three fee layers in mind, these questions keep you from loading a card whose costs only show up later. Ask them against the issuer’s official schedule, then use a comparison tool to sanity-check the totals.

Issuance and delivery

Is the first virtual card free in your country, or is there an application charge? Is a physical card optional, and what is the card fee plus delivery? As a concrete example from a current review, RedotPay lists a $10 virtual card fee and a $100 physical card fee, with identity checks at signup. Other issuers use different numbers — always re-read the official table for the programme you are considering.

Spending and conversion

  • What is the fee when you spend in the card’s default currency?
  • What is added when you spend in another currency?
  • Is there a separate conversion or “liquidation” charge when crypto is turned into the spending balance?
  • Are small transactions subject to a minimum fee?

ATM and extra reviews

ATM fees are often a flat amount plus a percentage. Some issuers also reserve the right to review large transactions, and a withdrawal can trigger an extra identity prompt. If cash access matters to you, read that section twice.

For current side-by-side maths, use the Crypto Card Fee Calculator and the fees comparison. If you expect to spend USDC often, cross-check the USDC debit card overview. For one worked issuance example, see the RedotPay review.
